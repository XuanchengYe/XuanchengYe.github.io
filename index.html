Hi, I'm

Xuancheng Ye

PhD Student in Data Science, University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill

I'm currently a PhD student in Data Science at UNC-Chapel Hill (2026–present). Before that, I completed my M.S. in Mathematics at the Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences, NYU (2023–2025), advised by Prof. Esteban G. Tabak and Prof. Siyu Heng. Before NYU, I earned my B.S. in Math & Applied Math at Southwestern University of Finance and Economics (2019–2023), advised by Prof. Chang Liu.

My research sits at the intersection of optimal transport, applied probability, optimization, and causal inference, with applications ranging from intervention optimization to multiphase optimization strategies (MOST).

Outside of research, I enjoy rock climbing and scuba diving.
